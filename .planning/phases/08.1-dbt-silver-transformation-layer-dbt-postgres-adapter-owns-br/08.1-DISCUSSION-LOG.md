**User's clarification on the run-claim question:** "Understanding and concept of Option 1 looks
good. However I am thinking at the moment about one DAG for sensoring the file on bucket, pulling
the data from bronze and processing it by dbt — with technics and capabilities expressed by
option 1 — to postgresql on silver, to finally process data between silver-postgres and
gold-postgres. Silver tables can have some metadata, technical columns for auditing and debugging.

confirmed Option 1 (dbt independent idempotency) as one connected DAG, and seeded the Silver
schema shape area's later questions (silver metadata columns, gold partitioning/indexing).

**Notes:** The incremental-key question was raised as a correctness concern discovered by
cross-referencing dbt's standard incremental pattern against this phase's own late-arriving-data
requirement — a naive `event_ts`-based filter would silently drop exactly the records this phase
exists to handle correctly.
