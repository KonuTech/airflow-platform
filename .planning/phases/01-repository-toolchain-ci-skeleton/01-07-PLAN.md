    decision must come from the body, not the first line.

    simultaneously. Its expectation states the exact field values after parsing, so a reader that
    handles each hazard alone but not together fails visibly.

    The escape-convention fixture covers both the doubled-quote and the backslash form. Its
    expectation states which convention the file uses and what the other interpretation would
    wrongly produce, because that difference is a data corruption, not a parse error.
