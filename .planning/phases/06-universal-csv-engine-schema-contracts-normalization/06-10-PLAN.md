<objective>
Build CSV-10's numeric half as a `StreamingStage`: locale-aware decimal/thousands-separator parsing,
accounting-convention negative styles (parentheses, trailing minus, leading minus), currency-symbol
and percent stripping, and two UNRECOVERABLE-damage rejections that must never be silently coerced —
spreadsheet scientific-notation identifier truncation and fixed-width leading-zero stripping. Every
parsed value is an exact `decimal.Decimal`, never a binary float.

Purpose: one of the independently-parallel normalizer plans. Corpus fixtures 20/21 (the
comma/point-locale pair), 50/51 (the two "this damage cannot be undone, reject it" cases — 06-CONTEXT.md's
own RESEARCH.md calls fixture 50 "THE MOST IMPORTANT DECLARATION IN THIS PLAN"), 57 (negative
conventions), 58 (currency/percent), and 59 (numeric null sentinels) each pin a distinct, named way a
naive numeric normalizer silently corrupts data — this plan's job is to make every one pass exactly.
`customers` has no numeric/currency columns (D-12's consequence note), so this capability's evidence is
its own corpus-fixture-driven tests.
Output: `NumericNormalizer`, unit-tested against every CSV-10-numeric-tagged corpus fixture.
</objective>
