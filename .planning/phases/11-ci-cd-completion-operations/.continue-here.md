- [ ] CONSTRAINT: `helm/values/ci/airflow.yaml` and `helm/values/local/airflow.yaml` must stay in sync on `config.scheduler.scheduler_health_check_threshold`, `config.dag_processor.dag_file_processor_timeout`, AND (as of this session) `config.core.parallelism` — these are BEHAVIORAL config, not a permitted resource-sizing divergence axis under `tests/policy/test_values_profiles.py`. - [ ] CONSTRAINT: Before trusting any live-CI conclusion about scheduler/dag-processor health, get DIRECT `kubectl get pods -o wide` / `kubectl describe pod` RESTARTS + `Last State`/`Reason` evidence — do not infer crash-loop status, OR its CAUSE (OOM vs. probe-timeout vs. admission-denial), from pytest failure messages alone. This session repeatedly found that the SAME pytest-level symptom ("discovery never registered it") was produced by at least 3 completely different underlying mechanisms across rounds (CPU/probe-threshold, memory/OOM, Kyverno admission denial).
- [ ] CONSTRAINT: A thoroughly offline-verified, well-reasoned fix can still have ZERO effect on live CI outcomes. Before concluding a fix worked (or didn't), diff the EXACT failing test node-IDs across the before/after runs — not just the pass/fail COUNT. This session hit byte-for-byte identical 17-test failure signatures across 8 consecutive live runs spanning 6 structurally different fixes; the count alone (17 failed every time) would have looked like "no progress" even when real, valuable component-level bugs (dag-processor OOM, a vault-0 race, a broken test fixture) were genuinely being fixed underneath it.
- [ ] CONSTRAINT: Perfect invariance of a failure signature across genuinely different, individually-confirmed fixes is itself a strong diagnostic signal — it means you are very likely fixing real-but-secondary problems while missing the actual proximate cause, or the actual cause is structural (resource ceiling vs. suite design) rather than a discrete bug. When you see this pattern, STOP mechanically iterating on "next plausible single-cause theory" and either (a) get instrumentation that produces DIRECT evidence of the actual mechanism (not another inference), or (b) surface the pattern to the user for a strategic decision, as this session did.

**Do not proceed until all boxes are checked.**
