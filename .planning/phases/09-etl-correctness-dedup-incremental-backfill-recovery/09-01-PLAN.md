<objective>
Remove the vestigial, never-consumed `deduplication:` block from both dataset YAMLs (dedup is
entirely dbt-owned since Phase 08.1, D-28), and add the config surface D-25's dataset-conditional
reconciliation sum-check needs (`reconciliation: {sum_columns: [...]}`), landing this FIRST because
later reconciliation-wiring plans (09-02, 09-07) read `ctx.config.reconciliation.sum_columns`.

Purpose: Close the config drift 08.1 left behind (a Pydantic-required field with no consumer) and
give VALID-05's sum check its dataset-declared input, without breaking the 13 existing test files
that construct `DeduplicationConfig` directly (RESEARCH.md Pitfall 4).
Output: `DatasetConfig.deduplication` becomes optional, `DatasetConfig.reconciliation` is new and
optional, both dataset YAMLs updated, full regression suite green.

**D-28 scope interpretation, confirmed explicitly (RESEARCH.md Assumption A1 flagged this as a
judgment call needing confirmation during planning):** D-28's literal wording is "remove the
vestigial `deduplication:` block" from the YAML — it does not say "purge `DeduplicationConfig` from
the codebase." This plan adopts the MINIMAL reading: make `DatasetConfig.deduplication` Optional and
delete the YAML block, keeping `DeduplicationConfig` itself and its 13 dependent test files
untouched. The alternative (fully purging `DeduplicationConfig`) would require coordinated edits to
all 13 test files listed in RESEARCH.md Pitfall 4 for zero behavior change — disproportionate to a
"cleanup" decision and not what D-28's own text asks for. This reading is adopted as final for
execution; if the fuller purge is later wanted, it is a separate, explicitly-scoped follow-up, not a
silent revision of this plan.
</objective>
