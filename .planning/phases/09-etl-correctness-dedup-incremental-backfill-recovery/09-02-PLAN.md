<objective>
Give `publish_ingest` two new same-transaction responsibilities: advancing the dataset's
observational watermark (D-01..D-04, INCR-01/02) and writing the silver→gold reconciliation row
(D-20/D-21/D-22/D-24, part of VALID-05). Both land in this ONE plan because both insert at the exact
same point in `publish_ingest`'s existing publish transaction (right after `publisher.publish()`
returns) — splitting them across two plans would mean two plans editing the same three lines of
`pipeline/run.py` in the same wave, an unsafe file-ownership conflict this plan avoids by
construction.

Purpose: INCR-02's "`>=`, never `>`" rule and VALID-05's "reported explicitly, quarantine-aware"
rule must both be enforced inside the SAME commit as the merge upsert (AP4 avoidance,
ARCHITECTURE.md line 1333) — a separate transaction could observe a partial publish.
Output: `meta.watermarks`, `meta.watermark_history`, `meta.reconciliation_results` tables;
`record_watermark`/`get_current_watermark`/`record_reconciliation` on the metadata repository;
`publish_ingest` wired to call both.
</objective>
