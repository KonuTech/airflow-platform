<objective>
Redesign generate_dated_series's customers-dataset generation from "N fresh customer_ids born
every day" to "a bounded, deterministic roster resent in full every day." This is a
prerequisite, not an enhancement: customers.yaml's source.change_semantics: snapshot (D-04,
activated for the first time this phase) means the SCD Publisher's DELETE-detection sweep (plan
10-03) treats each pass's file as the customer roster's full extent. The existing corpus (a fresh
block of new customer_ids every day, never repeating) would make every previously-known customer
look "vanished" on the second day, once the SCD Publisher becomes customers.yaml's real strategy
(plan 10-04) -- breaking the already-passing 2-year sweep test entirely, not just a new
assertion. Layer D-11's required anomalies (attribute change, late correction, missing customer)
AND D-06's required mass-delete/circuit-breaker-trip anomaly on top of the same roster model,
since a genuine "same customer across days" shape is exactly what all four anomalies need.

Purpose: give plan 10-07 a corpus that is simultaneously faithful to change_semantics: snapshot,
able to prove SCD-01/02/03/07/08 with a real repeated customer_id, able to prove D-06's mass-delete
circuit breaker against a real, deliberately-truncated snapshot (not just contrived integration-test
counts), and small (this repo's "2-year sweep" corpus is actually a 14-day representative-scale
corpus, not 730 days -- a bounded roster resent that many times is a trivial size increase).
Output: dated_series.py's customers path emits a full-roster daily snapshot with the required
anomaly days, including a mass-delete/circuit-breaker-trip day; the orders path is untouched (D-01
excludes orders from SCD).
</objective>
